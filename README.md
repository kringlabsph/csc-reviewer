# CSC Reviewer

A free reviewer for the Philippine Civil Service Exam (Professional and Subprofessional), in English and Filipino.

**Live site:** https://kringlabsph.github.io/csc-reviewer/

- Lessons for every topic
- Practice Q&A with explanations and formulas for math items
- Unlimited generated questions for Numerical, Analytical and Clerical topics
- Full-length timed mock exam (150 / 145 items) with a test booklet, answer sheet and report of rating
- Progress tracking, a 7-day study plan, and backup/restore of saved progress

The whole site is one file, `index.html`. Progress is saved in each visitor's browser.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Civil Service Commission.
